What it looks for

API keys and tokensOpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, GitHub, AWS, Google, Slack, JWTs, and long random strings that look like secrets
Secrets in configAnything after API_KEY=, token:, password= and friends
Contact detailsEmails and phone numbers
InfrastructureIPv4 addresses and card like numbers

Text recognition is not perfect. Always look at the result before you post. Your image is processed locally with Tesseract running in WebAssembly.
